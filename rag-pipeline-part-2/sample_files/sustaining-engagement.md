# Sustaining Long-Term Engagement

Recruiting volunteers is the easy part. Keeping them — through busy seasons,
setbacks, and the slow grind of ordinary weeks — is what separates lasting
organizations from short-lived efforts.

## Rotate Roles to Prevent Burnout

People burn out when they carry the same heavy responsibility indefinitely.
Build a rhythm where leaders step back and others step up. Rotation spreads the
load and quietly develops the next generation of organizers.

## Celebrate Small, Specific Wins

Vague praise fades; specific recognition sticks. Name the exact thing someone
did and the difference it made. Frequent, concrete appreciation does more for
retention than an annual banquet ever will.

## Build Rest Into the Calendar

Treat rest as part of the work, not a reward for finishing it. Teams that plan
deliberate pauses outlast teams that sprint until they collapse. A sustainable
pace, held over years, is what changes a community.
