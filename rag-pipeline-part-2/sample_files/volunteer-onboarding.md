# Onboarding New Volunteers

A volunteer's first day shapes whether they return. A warm, organized welcome
turns curious newcomers into committed contributors.

## Prepare Before They Arrive

Send a short welcome note before the first shift: where to park, who to ask
for, and what to wear. Removing small uncertainties lets new volunteers focus
on the work instead of logistics.

## Pair, Don't Drop

Assign every newcomer an experienced partner for their first few sessions.
People learn the culture of a place by watching someone who already belongs.
A good partner answers questions before they're asked and makes introductions.

## Give Real Work Early

Busywork signals that a volunteer isn't trusted. Hand newcomers a genuine,
achievable task on day one — something whose result they can see. Meaningful
contribution, however small, is what makes people want to come back.
